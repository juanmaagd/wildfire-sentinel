# 01 — Event Overview

## What it is

A one-day build hackathon by **+81 Builders** ("a build community, not a talk community").

- **Theme: Open.** Anything that uses AI and actually works. It does not have to be an agent.
- **Mindset from the organizers:** "Pick one real problem, decide early, make the strongest working product you can." "Working product > perfect product."
- Not production-ready software: "A small project that clearly solves one painful problem beats a complicated product with ten unfinished features."
- Capacity: 70 builders. Languages: Japanese and English (you only need one).

## Format

One-day team build → judging at your table → live demos from the top 5 → awards.

## Teams

- **1–4 people** per the Playbook (the Japanese Luma text says 1–6; the Playbook is the more recent source, so assume 1–4).
- Solo is allowed. Matchmaking at 10:30 for people without a team.

## Venue gotchas

- Good Space Kanda Tekko, **5th floor** (3-9-5 Uchikanda, Chiyoda City). Stairs only. The 4th floor is a similar space: wrong floor.
- **Close the entrance door every time** (neighbour noise complaints).
- **Power is scarce:** five extension cords and one cable reel for 70 people. Come charged, bring a battery.
- Wi-Fi: several routers, credentials shared at the venue. **Have phone tethering as backup.**
- No smoking anywhere, no loitering on stairs/entrance, sort the rubbish, leave the table clean.
