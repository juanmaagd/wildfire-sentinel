# 02 — Rules & Judging

## Mandatory rule

**Every team must use at least one sponsor tool** (Neo4j, ai&, or both). "Problem first. Tool second." Pick the ones that genuinely make the project better.

## Judging criteria (5 points each, 25 total)

1. **Real problem?** — Is this a problem people or companies really have?
2. **Does it work?** — Can they show us a working demo?
3. **Technically impressive?** — Does AI make the solution better, faster or easier?
4. **Good presentation?** — In 3 minutes, is it clear what it does and why it matters?
5. **Creative?** — Is the idea or approach new or surprising?

## Two rounds

**Round 1 — 16:00–16:25, at your table**
- Submitted teams are split into one group per judge.
- Each team: **3-minute demo + 1–2 min Q&A**, from your own laptop.
- Each judge picks **one** team from their group for the final. So you only need to beat the other teams in *your judge's* group.

**Final — 16:30, projector (100")**
- 5 teams, 3 min demo + 1 min Q&A each.
- Every judge ranks the five: 1st = 5 pts … 5th = 1 pt, summed. Round 1 scores don't carry over.
- Ties: ranked higher by more judges wins; then judges decide.
- Bring your own **HDMI adapter**; check the screen before 16:30.

## Submission (deadline 16:00, building stops)

One per team, via the submission form. Fields:

- Team name and all member names
- Email addresses of all members
- Project name and description
- **Code repository URL** (public, or invite the organizers)
- **Which sponsor tool you used and how**

Submit even if unfinished — every team that submits is judged.

## Suggested demo structure (from the Playbook)

1. **Problem** — what painful problem, for whom?
2. **Solution** — what did you build?
3. **Live demo** — show it working
4. **Why it matters** — impact, what's next

No slides required. If the demo is fragile, **have a backup recording or screenshots**.

## Prizes

- 1st place: revealed on the day.
- Other winners: Neo4j Aura credits (Luma also mentions ai& inference credits).
